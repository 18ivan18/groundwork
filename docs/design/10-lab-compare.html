Design notes

  • Green rows match the evidence pattern. Arrows show each passage's move from A to B; passages are matched by document and character overlap, since chunk ids differ between indexes.
  • Clicking a row expands the full chunk with its LLM context.
  • “Run as” applies that user's ACL, so restricted documents can be checked from here.
  • The question and settings are in the URL; a comparison can be linked from a pull request.

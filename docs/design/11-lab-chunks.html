Design notes

  • Chunks are aligned by character offset, so the same text sits side by side; scrolling one side scrolls the other.
  • Semantic chunks show the distance at which they were cut; window chunks show their overlap shaded.
  • LLM-written context is shown in italics above the chunk. It is indexed but never cited or shown in answers.
  • Reachable from a document's drawer (“View chunks side by side”) and from Compare.

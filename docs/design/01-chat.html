Hotel limits on business trips

How much can I spend on a hotel night when I travel for work?

Under the current expense policy, a hotel night is reimbursed up to €150, or €220 in cities on the high-cost list 1. Bookings above the limit need your manager's approval before travel 2.

An older version of the policy, dated June 2024, set a single limit of €120; it was replaced in March 2026 3.

# Remote work guidelines

Northwind Labs has offices in Rotterdam and Kraków, and most teams work partly from home. These
guidelines keep a distributed team working well together.

## Core hours

Be available between **10:00 and 15:00 CET** on working days. Outside core hours, plan your day as
you like, but keep your calendar up to date.

## Home office

- Every employee can claim a one-off **home office allowance of €600** for a desk, chair or screen.
  Claim it through the expense tool within the first six months.
- Company laptops must be used on a private network or with the VPN on. Public Wi-Fi without the
  VPN is not allowed.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to **30 days per calendar year**. Tell your manager and
the people team at least **two weeks** before you go: tax and insurance rules differ by country.

## Office days

Teams choose at least one shared office day per week. New joiners spend their first two weeks in the
office where possible.

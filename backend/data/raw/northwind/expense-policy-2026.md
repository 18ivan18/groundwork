# Travel and expense policy

Effective **1 February 2026**. Applies to everyone at Northwind Labs who travels or spends money on
the company's behalf.

## Daily allowance for meals

You can claim up to **€55 per day** for meals while travelling. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Accommodation

- Up to **€180 per night**.
- Up to **€220 per night** in capital cities.

## Flights and trains

- Economy class by default.
- **Premium economy** is allowed for flights longer than **six hours**.
- For trips under four hours, take the train.

## Claims

- Attach a receipt for **every** expense, whatever the amount.
- Submit claims within **30 days**.
- Spending above **€1,000** needs your manager's approval in advance.

## Questions

Ask in #ask-finance or read the FAQ in the expense tool.

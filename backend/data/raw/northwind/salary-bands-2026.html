How bands are used

  • New hires are offered a salary between the minimum and the midpoint of their band. Offers above the midpoint need approval (see Hiring approvals and offer process).
  • Nobody is paid below the minimum of their band. If a band's minimum rises above someone's salary, their salary is raised on 1 January.
  • Promotions move a person into the next band with a raise of at least 8%.

# Hiring approvals and offer process

This process applies to every permanent role at Northwind Labs.

## Opening a role

- A new role needs **headcount approval** from the CFO, Tomasz Brandt, and the head of the
  department. Replacing someone who left needs only the head of the department.
- The hiring manager writes the job description with a recruiter and sets the level.

## Interviews

Engineering interviews have four stages: a recruiter call, a technical conversation, a work sample
discussed with two engineers, and a values interview with someone from another team.

## Making an offer

- Offers are made within the band for the level and office (see *Engineering salary bands 2026*).
- An offer **up to the band midpoint** needs the hiring manager and the recruiter.
- An offer **above the midpoint** also needs the department's VP.
- An offer **above the band maximum** is not allowed. Change the level instead, if the candidate's
  experience justifies it.

## Before the start date

- Two **reference checks**, at least one from a former manager.
- The contract is signed by the head of people.
- IT is told at least **ten working days** before the start date so the laptop is ready.

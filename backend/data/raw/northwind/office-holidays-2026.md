# Office holidays 2026

Both offices are closed on these days in 2026. Local public holidays that are not on the list are
normal working days; you can take them as annual leave.

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| 1 January | Thursday | New Year's Day |
| 3 April | Friday | Good Friday |
| 6 April | Monday | Easter Monday |
| 1 May | Friday | Company day |
| 24 December | Thursday | Christmas Eve (afternoon only) |
| 25 December | Friday | Christmas Day |
| 31 December | Thursday | New Year's Eve (afternoon only) |

## Notes

- Afternoon-only days end at **13:00**.
- There are no production deploys on the working day before an office holiday (see *Deploying to
  production*).
- On-call continues on holidays. If you are on call on a full holiday, you get one extra day of
  leave.

# Vendor payment runbook

Northwind Labs pays vendor invoices in two **payment runs a week**, on **Tuesday** and **Thursday**.

## Before a payment run

1. Check that each invoice matches an approved purchase order and that the goods or services were
   received.
2. Invoices up to **€10,000** need one approver from finance.
3. Invoices above **€10,000** need **two approvers**: one from finance and the budget owner.

## Changes to bank details

Changes to a vendor's bank details are the most common way of defrauding a company. For any change:

- Never accept a change by email alone, even from a known contact.
- Call the vendor back on the phone number already in the vendor record, not one in the email.
- A second person from finance checks the call-back and approves the change.
- Hold the first payment to the new account until the vendor confirms receipt of a **€1 test
  payment**.

## Running the payment

The payment file is generated in the finance portal and uploaded to the bank by one person and
released by another. Nobody can do both.

## Late invoices

Invoices received after **12:00 CET** on a payment day go into the next run. Urgent payments outside
a run need the finance controller's approval.

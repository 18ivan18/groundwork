# Expense policy

Effective **1 March 2024**. This policy applies to all Northwind Labs employees who spend money on
the company's behalf, for example when travelling to a customer or to the other office.

## Meals

- While travelling, meals are reimbursed up to **€40 per day**.
- Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Hotels

- Hotels are reimbursed up to **€150 per night**.
- Book through the travel portal where possible.

## Travel

- Book economy class for all flights.
- Trains are preferred for trips under four hours.

## Receipts and claims

- Keep a receipt for every expense above **€25**. Below that, a note in the claim is enough.
- Submit claims in the expense tool within **60 days** of the expense.
- Expenses above **€500** need your manager's approval before you spend the money.

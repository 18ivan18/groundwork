# Reporting a security incident

Report anything that might be a security problem, even if you are not sure. A false alarm costs us a
few minutes; an unreported breach can cost us our customers' trust.

## Examples

- You clicked a link in a suspicious email or entered your password on an unexpected page.
- A laptop or phone with Northwind Labs data was lost or stolen.
- You see credentials, tokens or customer data in a public place, a log or a chat channel.
- A customer reports seeing another customer's data.

## How to report

- Post in **#security-reports** within **one hour** of noticing. Say what happened and when. Do not
  paste the secret or the customer data itself.
- Outside working hours, page the **security on-call** through Beacon.
- A lost device must also be reported to IT within **24 hours** (see *Laptop and device policy*).

## What happens next

The security on-call decides whether it is a security incident. If customer data may be exposed,
the incident is a **SEV1** and the head of legal is informed, because some customers' contracts
require us to notify them within **72 hours**.

Do not try to investigate on your own: do not log in to affected accounts or delete anything. The
security team needs the evidence as it is.

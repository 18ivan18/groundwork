# Disciplinary procedure

This procedure is for managers and the people team. It is used when an employee's conduct, not
their performance, is a problem. Performance problems follow the *Performance review process*.

## Principles

- A people partner is involved from the first formal step.
- The employee is told in writing what the concern is, and can respond before any decision.
- The employee may bring a colleague to every formal meeting.

## Steps

1. **Informal conversation:** the manager raises the concern and agrees on what must change. A note
   is kept by the manager, not in the personnel file.
2. **Written warning:** if the conduct continues. Stays in the personnel file for **12 months**.
3. **Final written warning:** if it continues after a written warning. Stays in the file for
   **24 months**.
4. **Dismissal:** decided by the head of people together with the department head.

Serious misconduct, such as theft, violence or deliberately exposing customer data, can lead
directly to step 4 after an investigation.

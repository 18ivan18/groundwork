# Payroll run checklist

Shared by the finance and people teams. Northwind Labs pays salaries on the **25th of each month**.
If the 25th is on a weekend or an office holiday, salaries are paid on the working day before.

## Cut-off

Changes for the month must reach payroll by the **15th**: new joiners, leavers, salary changes,
parental leave and unpaid leave. Changes after the 15th are paid in the next month's run, with a
correction.

## Steps

1. **By the 16th:** the people team sends the list of changes to finance.
2. **By the 18th:** finance enters the changes in the payroll provider's system.
3. **By the 20th:** the payroll provider sends the draft payroll. A person from finance and a
   person from the people team each check it against the list of changes. Any salary that differs
   by more than **5%** from the previous month needs an explanation.
4. **By the 22nd:** the finance controller approves the payroll.
5. **On the 23rd:** the payment file is released to the bank, so salaries arrive on the 25th.

## Confidentiality

Payroll files contain everyone's salary. Keep them in the payroll folder only; never send them by
chat or email attachment.

# Parental leave: administration guide

This guide is for the people team. It explains how to process parental leave at Northwind Labs.
Employees read the short version in the employee handbook.

## Entitlement

- **Birth parent:** 16 weeks of fully paid leave.
- **Other parent, including adoptive parents:** 8 weeks of fully paid leave, to be taken within the
  first year after the birth or adoption.
- Statutory leave in the Netherlands and Poland comes on top where it is longer; the people team
  checks the local rules for each case.

## Processing a request

1. The employee tells their manager and the people team at least **ten weeks** before the leave
   starts.
2. The people team confirms the dates in writing within **five working days**.
3. Payroll is told by the **15th of the month** before the leave starts, so the payroll run is
   correct (see *Payroll run checklist*).
4. The manager plans cover; on-call duties stop from the first day of leave.

## Returning

Employees return to the same role. They can work **80% of their hours at full pay** for the first
four weeks back.

# Performance review process

Performance reviews at Northwind Labs happen **twice a year**, in **April** and **October**. This
guide is for managers and the people team.

## Timeline

1. **Week 1:** employees write a self-review; managers collect feedback from two or three peers.
2. **Week 2:** managers write the review and propose a rating.
3. **Week 3:** calibration. Managers of the same department meet with a people partner to compare
   ratings across teams.
4. **Week 4:** managers hold review conversations and share the written review.

## Ratings

| Rating | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1 | Does not meet expectations |
| 2 | Partly meets expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations |
| 5 | Far exceeds expectations |

Ratings are not shared outside the employee, their manager and the people team.

## Consequences

- A rating of **4 or 5** in October makes the employee eligible for a promotion discussion.
- Two ratings of **1 or 2** in a row lead to a performance improvement plan (PIP), agreed with the
  people team. A PIP lasts **60 days** and has weekly check-ins.
- Salary reviews happen once a year, in January, and take the October rating into account.

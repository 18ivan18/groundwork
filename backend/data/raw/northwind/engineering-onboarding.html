Engineering onboarding checklist

Welcome to Northwind Labs. This checklist covers your first eight weeks as an engineer. Your onboarding buddy goes through it with you; tick items off in your onboarding ticket.

Day 1

  • Collect your laptop from IT and turn on full-disk encryption before you sign in to anything else.
  • Join #engineering, #deploys, #incidents and your team's channel.
  • Request staging access and Lantern dashboards through the access form. Both are approved automatically.

Week 1

  • Run Tern locally and ship a small change to staging with your buddy.
  • Read the Incident response runbook and Incident severity levels.
  • Attend the weekly operations review on Wednesday at 14:00 CET.

Weeks 2 to 4

  • Make your first production deploy through Shipyard, with your buddy watching.
  • Pair on at least two code reviews.

Weeks 5 to 8

  • Shadow one full week of on-call: you receive the pages but the primary responds.
  • After week 8 you join your team's on-call rotation (see the On-call handbook).

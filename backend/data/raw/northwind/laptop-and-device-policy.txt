LAPTOP AND DEVICE POLICY
Northwind Labs IT
Effective 12 May 2025

1. Company laptops
Every employee gets a company laptop. Laptops are replaced every three years, or sooner if they are
broken beyond repair.

2. Security settings
Full-disk encryption must be on. The screen must lock after five minutes without use. Operating
system updates must be installed within seven days of release. IT checks these settings every day
and blocks access to company systems from laptops that fail them.

3. Personal use
Reasonable personal use is allowed. Do not install software from unknown sources, and do not let
anyone else use the laptop, including family members.

4. Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT within 24 hours, and post in #security-reports within
one hour if it held customer data. IT wipes the device remotely.

5. Leaving Northwind Labs
Return the laptop on your last working day. IT wipes it before it is given to someone else.

Questions: #it-help
